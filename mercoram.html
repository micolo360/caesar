The job hunt is a sales pipeline

Engineers and designers looking for work send dozens of applications and cold emails, then lose track of who replied, who needs a nudge and when the interview is. Mercoram treats that like a sales pipeline: write a cold email once, personalise it per company, send it from your own Gmail and follow every application to an offer.

The site needed to explain that in seconds and make the product feel real before it fully exists.

A headline you can’t misread

“Send the email. Land the offer.” is the whole product in six words. It sits over a soft sky-blue scene with clouds, which keeps a stressful topic feeling light and hopeful. Satoshi is used across the whole site for a modern, friendly tone.

Show the product, don’t describe it

Right under the hero, a full product dashboard shows what you would actually use: tabs for applications, cold emails, follow-ups, interviews and templates; a board with columns for each stage; a weekly calendar; and a cold-email draft with a “Send from Gmail” button.

Seeing the board makes the promise believable in a way a list of features never could.

Features as small stories

Each feature gets a card with a mini interface inside it: cold emails with variables for name, company and role; automatic nudges on day 3 and day 7; interviews kept with the right application; a drag-and-drop board; privacy by default; and a live feed of replies.

Pricing that lowers the risk

Three tiers make the decision easy: Starter is free forever, Pro is $29 a month and Team is $59 a month for coaches and bootcamps. The paid plans are marked “coming soon” with a waitlist, so the page converts people today without overpromising.

The FAQ answers the honest questions, including how emails are sent and whether data is encrypted, and the page closes with “Give every application a paper trail.” Every single button on the site leads somewhere: a section, a sign-up or a waitlist.

What went live

  • Product positioning, headline and page structure
  • Responsive landing site with an interactive product dashboard
  • Six feature cards, three-tier pricing, FAQ and newsletter
  • Hover states on every button, deployed on Vercel
