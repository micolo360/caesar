Beforeyou ask

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from $500. The final price depends on the number of pages, features and how much content needs creating. After a project call you get a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. You get a timeline with your quote, and I share progress as I go so you always know where things stand.

Do you build in code or in Framer?

Both. Framer is great when you want to edit pages yourself. Hand-coded builds suit custom 3D, WebGL and heavy interaction. I’ll recommend whichever fits your project.

Can you work with my time zone?

Yes. I’m on West Africa Time (GMT+1), which overlaps a full working day with the UK and Europe and the morning with the US. My calendar shows call times in your own time zone.

What do you need from me to start?

Your goals, who the site is for, any brand assets you already have, and a few sites you like. If you don’t have a brand yet, I can design one with you.

What happens after launch?

You get everything handed over and a walkthrough of how to update your site. I’m available for changes, new pages and features whenever you need them.
