Fintech sites all look the same

Most banking websites use the same blue gradients, phone mockups and stock smiles. Moniepoint powers payments for millions of Nigerian businesses, from market stalls to supermarkets, and I wanted to see if a site could feel as cultural and confident as that story.

This is a self-initiated concept. It is clearly labelled as unofficial, every button links to the real moniepoint.com, and the page is hidden from search engines.

Classical paintings, Nigerian business

The art direction borrows from Renaissance paintings: painted hands, marble columns and gold. In the hero, two painted hands hold phones while gold coins arc endlessly from one to the other, a simple picture of money moving instantly.

I kept Moniepoint’s own blue for every action button, so the brand stays recognisable inside the new look.

Showing the whole business in one view

“One bank for the whole business” is shown with an illustrative dashboard: balance, today’s sales, instant settlement, loan limit and a split of payment channels. A business switcher shows several shops in different cities managed from one place.

Every figure is marked “illustrative data,” because a concept should never pretend to be a real account.

Products as museum pieces

Each product got its own piece of custom art in the same painted style: a gold POS terminal (“POS machine wey no get wahala”), a gold key for the business account, a card for expenses and a coin for working capital loans.

The company numbers, 6M+ businesses, 16M banked customers, 14B+ transactions and ₦1T+ in loans, count up as they appear, with a note crediting the public 2025 reports they come from.

Ending on building

A customer story about a business owner who rebuilt after a robbery adds the human side. The closing section shows a painted hand placing coins on a rising brick wall under “Let’s build a stronger foundation for your business,” followed by four simple steps to open an account.

What went live

  • Concept art direction and custom illustrations: POS, key, card and coin
  • Responsive single-page site with a looping coin hero and animated counters
  • Illustrative dashboard, customer story and onboarding steps
  • Clear unofficial-concept labelling, deployed on Vercel
