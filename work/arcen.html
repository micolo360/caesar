Making a chair feel touchable online

Arcen is a furniture brand I created to explore one question: how do you make someone want a chair they cannot sit in? Premium furniture sells on materials and feel, and most furniture sites flatten that into a grid of photos and prices.

I designed the landing page in Figma first, then built the whole store by hand so every interaction could be tuned.

An olive world for warm wood

The whole site sits on a deep olive green. It makes walnut, oak and bouclé glow, and it feels like a calm room rather than a shop. Type is clean and quiet so the chairs do the talking.

Telling the story of one chair

Instead of listing features in a table, the signature pedestal chair floats in the middle of the page with hotspots that point to what matters: the padded cushion, the backrest, the balanced base and the solid wood.

Further down, “More than a chair” and “Designed for your everyday” sell the life around the furniture: slow mornings, long conversations, quiet evenings.

A shop that works, not a mockup

Arcen is a working multi-page store, not just a landing page. The shop has filters for everything, new arrivals, best sellers, lounge and dining chairs. Product pages let you pick a frame finish, change quantity and add to a cart that slides in from the side.

Small details carry the premium feel: made-to-order lead times, free delivery and placement, and a ten-year structural guarantee shown right under the Add to cart button where buyers need reassurance most.

Every page has a job

Our Story starts with “We started with one chair and a stubborn question,” giving the brand a human origin. The sign-up page invites people into the Arcen letter with a friendly line about “the occasional chair that nearly worked.”

Scroll animations, micro-interactions and hover effects run through every page, with Apple-style restraint: things move to guide attention, never just to show off.

What went live

  • Brand direction and landing page design in Figma
  • Hand-coded five-page store: home, shop, product detail, our story, sign-up
  • Filters, product options, a working cart and newsletter sign-up
  • Scroll and hover animation throughout, responsive on every device, deployed on Netlify
